# Calculus on manifolds: a modern approach to classical theorems of advanced calculus

> [!info] Spivak, Michael · 2018 · CRC Press, Taylor \& Francis Group

## Summary
> [!note] AI-drafted from the bibliographic record (a classic textbook; no abstract) — a base to refine.
**TL;DR** — Spivak's compact classic textbook developing multivariable calculus rigorously up to differential forms and the generalized Stokes' theorem on manifolds.
**Problem** — Presenting the classical theorems of advanced calculus (the divergence, Green's, and Stokes' theorems) in a modern, unified, coordinate-free framework.
**Method** — Builds from functions on Euclidean space (differentiation, the inverse/implicit function theorems, integration) to the calculus of differential forms and integration on chains and manifolds, culminating in the single generalized Stokes' theorem.
**Key results** — Unifies the vector-calculus integral theorems as special cases of the generalized Stokes' theorem, ∫_∂M ω = ∫_M dω. (Textbook.)

## Takeaways
- Standard reference for differential forms, exterior derivative, and coordinate-free integration on manifolds.
- Provides the manifold/differential-geometry vocabulary that geometric nonlinear control (Lie derivatives, distributions) presupposes.
