> [!info] R. Abraham; J. E. Marsden; T. Ratiu · 1983 · Addison-Wesley

## Summary

**TL;DR** — A rigorous graduate reference on the analysis and geometry of manifolds — differential and tensor calculus on manifolds, vector fields and flows, differential forms, and applications to dynamical systems.
**Problem** — Provides the self-contained mathematical foundations (calculus on Banach spaces and manifolds) needed to do global analysis and geometric mechanics rigorously.
**Method** — A textbook development: from Banach-space calculus and the inverse/implicit function theorems up through manifold theory, vector bundles, tensor fields, Lie derivatives, differential forms, integration on manifolds, and Stokes' theorem, with applications.
**Key results** — Not a results paper; a standard reference (Abraham, Marsden, Ratiu) for the differential-geometric and tensor-analytic machinery underlying geometric control and mechanics.
