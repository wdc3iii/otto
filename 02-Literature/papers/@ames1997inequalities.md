# Inequalities for differential and integral equations

> [!info] Ames, William F; Pachpatte, BG · 1997 · Elsevier

## Summary
> [!note] AI-drafted from the publisher/catalog description (no abstract; this is a research monograph) — a base to refine.
**TL;DR** — A research monograph (B. G. Pachpatte; W. F. Ames is the *Mathematics in Science and Engineering* series editor) collecting integral and differential inequalities of the Gronwall–Bellman–Bihari–Pachpatte type and their use in the qualitative theory of differential and integral equations.
**Problem** — Bounding, and establishing existence/uniqueness/stability/boundedness of, solutions to differential and integral equations requires a systematic toolbox of a-priori inequalities.
**Method** — Develops and generalizes linear and nonlinear integral inequalities (Gronwall-type and their many extensions) and shows how they yield estimates on solutions and on the difference between solutions of related equations.
**Key results** — A reference compendium of such inequalities with applications; not an empirical paper, so results are theorems/estimates rather than measured numbers.

## Takeaways
- The core tool: a Gronwall-type inequality turns an integral bound on a quantity into an explicit (often exponential) closed-form bound — the standard route to trajectory/error growth estimates.
- Value is as a lookup reference for the exact inequality variant needed in a proof, rather than a single result.
- Cited for machinery, not findings; the relevant content is whichever inequality a downstream proof invokes.
