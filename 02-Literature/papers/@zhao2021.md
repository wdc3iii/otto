## Abstract
This paper presents an approach towards guaranteed trajectory tracking for nonlinear control-affine systems subject to external disturbances based on robust control contraction metrics (CCM) that aims to minimize the $\mathcal L_\infty$ gain from the disturbances to nominal-actual trajectory deviations. The guarantee is in the form of invariant tubes, computed offline and valid for any nominal trajectories, in which the actual states and inputs of the system are guaranteed to stay despite disturbances.
