## Summary
> [!note] AI-drafted from the abstract — a base to refine.
**TL;DR** — A concise, self-contained graduate textbook that builds optimal control theory from the calculus of variations, through the maximum principle, to HJB dynamic programming and LQR.
**Problem** — A one-semester, rigorous-but-compact introduction to optimal control for engineering and applied-math graduate students.
**Method** — Starts with the calculus of variations to lay groundwork, then gives a complete proof of the Pontryagin maximum principle and covers Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman dynamic programming and linear-quadratic optimal control, with historical notes and exercises per chapter.
**Key results** — A textbook, not a research result; its value is the clean, self-contained derivation path from variational calculus to the maximum principle, HJB, and LQR.

## Abstract (from bib)
This textbook offers a concise yet rigorous introduction to calculus of variations and optimal control theory, and is a self-contained resource for graduate students in engineering, applied mathematics, and related subjects. Designed specifically for a one-semester course, the book begins with calculus of variations, preparing the ground for optimal control.
