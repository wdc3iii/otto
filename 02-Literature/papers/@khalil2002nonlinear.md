# Nonlinear systems; 3rd ed.

> [!info] Khalil, Hassan K · 2002 · The book can be consulted by contacting: PH-AID: Wallet, Lionel

## Summary
> [!note] AI-drafted description of a canonical textbook (no abstract) — a base to refine.
**TL;DR** — Khalil's *Nonlinear Systems* (3rd ed., Prentice Hall, 2002) is the standard graduate reference for nonlinear dynamical systems and control.
**Problem** — Provide a rigorous, self-contained treatment of the analysis and control of nonlinear systems for graduate students and researchers.
**Method** — A textbook, not a research paper: it develops the theory of nonlinear ordinary differential systems and their control from foundations through advanced design.
**Key results** — Standard coverage includes existence/uniqueness and qualitative behavior of solutions, Lyapunov stability theory (autonomous and time-varying), the invariance principle, comparison and input-to-state stability, input-output and passivity-based analysis, and nonlinear control design (feedback linearization, Lyapunov-based and backstepping-style design).
