# Feedback Systems: An Introduction for Scientists and Engineers

> [!info] Astrom, Karl Johan; Murray, Richard M. · 2008 · Princeton University Press

## Summary
> [!note] AI-drafted from the abstract — a base to refine.
**TL;DR** — A discipline-agnostic introduction to feedback control, teaching modeling, analysis, and design from a state-space, dynamical-systems viewpoint.
**Problem** — A self-contained textbook on the mathematics of feedback accessible across physical, biological, information, and economic systems.
**Method** — Develops transfer functions via the exponential response of a system and introduces control-oriented modeling using tools from physics, computer science, and operations research. Begins with state-space tools for analysis and design — stability of solutions, Lyapunov functions, reachability, state feedback — and proceeds to frequency-domain and robustness topics.
**Key results** — A textbook; its contribution is a modern, cross-disciplinary treatment grounding feedback design in state-space and Lyapunov analysis.
