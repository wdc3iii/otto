# A primer on b\'ezier curves

> [!info] Kamermans, M. · 2020 · (online book)

## Summary
> [!note] AI-drafted from the online book's front matter (no abstract) — a base to refine.
**TL;DR** — A free online book (Pomax) covering both the mathematics and the programming of Bézier curves, from first principles to practical algorithms.
**Problem** — Bézier curves appear everywhere (drawing paths, font outlines, CSS easing) but practitioners need an accessible, worked treatment of the math and its implementation.
**Method** — Interactive ebook that develops Bézier curves and works through a wide range of topics for drawing with and manipulating them (evaluation, derivatives, curve fitting, offsetting, etc.).
**Key results** — Not a research paper; a reference resource, so it is cited for the standard Bézier constructions rather than a result.

## Takeaways
- Reference for Bézier curve math and algorithms, not an original research contribution.
- Cited when trajectories or splines are parameterized as Bézier curves (control-point-based, differentiable, easy to constrain).

## Relevance to your work
Bézier parameterization is the trajectory representation used when planning dynamically feasible motions with control-point constraints. See [[@csomayshanklin2025dynamically]].
