# Summer Thoughts on Autonomy

An informal, recorded **lecture series** thinking out loud about the questions, tools, and
methods behind robot autonomy — how we decide a problem is "solved," the simulators we
validate in, and the optimization machinery under modern control. Published to
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLYrX9d3is5XrHpwcX6Q9oIr7EaGn3TyoU) over
summer 2026; new lectures added as the series continues.
